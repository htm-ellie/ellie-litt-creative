About me

A cross-disciplinary creative spanning print, code, and sound.

ELLIE LITT

Born and raised in South Florida, I’m a first-generation Cuban American, deeply influenced by culture, rhythm, and craft. I bring my heart—and my sazón—to every project I touch, no matter the medium.

I work across web design, front-end development, and SEO to build digital experiences that are as functional as they are expressive. When I’m not writing code, I’m carving relief prints and hand-crafting designs through my printmaking company, Little Cosas. Music is my longest-running obsession—I’m a lifelong pianist who has recorded solo work and collaborated with bands and other artists.

Every medium I work in feeds the others, shaping a practice rooted in curiosity, craft, and intentional design.
